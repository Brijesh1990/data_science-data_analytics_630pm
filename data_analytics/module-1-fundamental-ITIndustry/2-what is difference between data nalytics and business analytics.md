# Difference Between Data Analytics and Business Analytics

**Data analytics** is the process of examining data to find patterns, answer questions, and support decisions. It can be used in many fields, including healthcare, science, education, government, and business.

**Business analytics** applies data analysis, statistical methods, and business knowledge to understand business performance and guide business decisions. It is focused on questions such as how to increase sales, reduce costs, improve customer retention, or plan resources.

## Key Differences

| Area | Data Analytics | Business Analytics |
|---|---|---|
| **Main focus** | Discovering and communicating useful findings from data | Solving business problems and improving business decisions |
| **Scope** | Broad; applies to many fields and types of questions | Specifically concerned with organizations and business goals |
| **Typical questions** | What patterns or relationships are present in this data? | What is affecting business performance, and what action should the organization take? |
| **Data used** | Any relevant data, such as scientific, medical, public, or commercial data | Business data, such as sales, costs, customers, inventory, and operations |
| **Success measured by** | Quality and usefulness of the analysis and findings | Business outcomes, such as revenue, efficiency, customer satisfaction, or risk reduction |

## Example

A data analyst might examine a dataset of customer purchases to identify buying patterns. A business analyst might use those findings, together with business goals and constraints, to recommend a promotion or a change to product inventory.

## How They Relate

The two fields overlap: business analytics often uses data analytics techniques. The main distinction is their **purpose and context**. Data analytics is the broader process of analyzing data; business analytics applies that process to business questions and decisions. In some organizations, the job titles and responsibilities overlap.

**In short:** data analytics asks what the data can tell us; business analytics focuses on how those findings can help a business decide what to do.
